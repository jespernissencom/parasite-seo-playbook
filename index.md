## The social workflow from the blog (free preview)

From [Parasite SEO with social media](https://jespernissen.com/parasite-seo-with-social-media/), the loop after you publish an article is:

1. Publish the article on your site. Put the target keyword in the H1.
2. Make a YouTube video on that keyword. Link the article in the description.
3. Make a YouTube Community post about the article. Add an image.
4. Publish an X Article (or a tweet if you are not Premium) with links to the video and the article.
5. Post on your Facebook Page with an image.
6. Instagram post with an image.
7. Threads post with an image and a link.
8. Reddit post with **original** wording. Best on a subreddit you control.
9. Cut a Short from the video. Link to the long video.
10. Repost the short-form clip to TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Pinterest.
11. Pinterest board + pin that points back to the article.
12. LinkedIn post about the article with a link.

Jesper’s line from that article: if you follow the workflow, you are aiming at **multiple page-one positions**, not one lucky ranking. That is the whole game.

The playbook goes past this list. It covers how to write the post on each network, how to daisy-chain the URLs, how to index them, how press releases and cloud/foundation links sit on top, and how to do this on a weekday schedule instead of a weekend binge.

## How to structure the posts (from the same article)

- On **Facebook, Instagram, and Threads**, put the keyword in the **first words** of the post. Those networks often turn the opening line into the title Google shows.
- Do not stuff every post with a pile of links. Social sites are twitchy about that.
- **X Articles** beat normal tweets when you can use them. Long-form on x.com is treated more like a page than a 200-character status.
- **Reddit** wants original text. Do not paste the Facebook caption into a subreddit and hope.
- One branded hashtag (example from the blog: a name-tag like `#jespernissenseo`) is for the platform algorithm and brand search, not a substitute for the keyword in the first line.
- A short X post can be expanded into the longer Facebook / LinkedIn version. Do not clone blindly.

The playbook chapter “The anatomy of a ranking post” plus the per-platform chapters (LinkedIn, Facebook, X, YouTube, Reddit, Instagram/Threads/Pinterest) is where this is written out as checklists.
