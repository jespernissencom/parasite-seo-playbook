## The problem

A new domain takes months or years to compete. Meanwhile page one is full of YouTube videos, LinkedIn posts, Facebook pages, Reddit threads, and press pages that already have authority.

Parasite SEO is publishing on those properties on purpose, so your offer shows up in Google without waiting for your own site to mature.

## What this playbook is

The exact process Jesper Nissen uses: social media + Web 2.0 + Google properties + press releases.

You get the posting order, the platform lists, the prompts, and the step-by-step procedures. Not a motivation video. A field manual.
